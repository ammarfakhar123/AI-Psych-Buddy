# General Wellbeing Habits

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## Building a steady routine
Small, regular habits tend to be easier to keep than big changes. Choose one habit and make it very small, such as a ten-minute walk.

## Areas many people find supportive
- **Movement.** Gentle activity you enjoy, such as walking, dancing or stretching.
- **Rest.** Regular sleep and short breaks during the day.
- **Connection.** Time with people who make you feel safe and understood.
- **Meaning and enjoyment.** Hobbies, nature, music, creativity or volunteering.
- **Nourishment.** Regular meals and staying hydrated.

## Being kind to yourself
Speak to yourself the way you would to a good friend. Mistakes and hard days are part of being human. Notice small wins.

## Feeling lonely or low
Reaching out, even with a short message to someone you trust, can help. If low feelings last or make daily life difficult, consider contacting a qualified mental-health professional.

# Journaling for Self-Reflection

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## Why journal
Putting thoughts into words can help you slow down, notice patterns and feel a little more clear. There is no right or wrong way to journal.

## Simple ways to start
- **Free writing.** Set a timer for five minutes and write whatever comes to mind without editing.
- **Three good things.** Write down three things that went okay today, however small.
- **Feelings check-in.** Complete the sentences "Right now I feel..." and "I think this is because..."
- **Brain dump.** Write every task and worry on paper to get them out of your head.

## Reflection prompts
What was the hardest part of today? What helped, even a little? What do I need right now? What would I say to a friend feeling this way? What is one thing I'm looking forward to?

## Tips
Keep it private and kind. Skip days without guilt. If writing about something feels too heavy, pause and do a grounding or breathing exercise instead.

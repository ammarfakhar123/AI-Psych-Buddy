# 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Exercise

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## What grounding is
Grounding exercises are simple techniques that bring your attention to the present moment using your senses. Many people find them useful when their thoughts feel fast, scattered or overwhelming.

## The 5-4-3-2-1 steps
Take a slow breath, then move through your senses:

- **5 things you can see.** Look around and name five things, such as a lamp, a plant or a patch of light on the wall.
- **4 things you can touch.** Notice the texture of your clothes, the chair beneath you, or the temperature of a mug.
- **3 things you can hear.** Listen for distant traffic, a fan, birds or your own breathing.
- **2 things you can smell.** Notice any scents nearby, or recall two smells you enjoy.
- **1 thing you can taste.** Notice the taste in your mouth, or sip some water.

## Tips
Go slowly and say each item out loud or in your head. If your mind wanders, gently return to the sense you were on. You can repeat the exercise as many times as you like.

## Other quick grounding ideas
Press your feet into the floor and notice the pressure. Hold something cold, like an ice cube or a cold glass of water. Name the colours you can see in the room. Stretch your arms slowly and notice how your muscles feel.

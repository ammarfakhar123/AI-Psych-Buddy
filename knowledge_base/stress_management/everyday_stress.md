# Everyday Stress Management

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## Noticing stress
Stress can show up as tense muscles, a racing mind, irritability or feeling overwhelmed. Noticing early signs can help you respond sooner.

## Small things that many people find helpful
- **Break tasks down.** Write down everything on your mind, pick the one most important item, and split it into a small first step.
- **Take short breaks.** Stand up, stretch, drink water or step outside for five minutes.
- **Move your body.** A short walk is an easy way to release tension.
- **Set gentle boundaries.** It is okay to say no or to ask for more time.
- **Connect with others.** Talking to someone you trust can make a problem feel more manageable.

## Sorting what you can control
Draw two columns: "I can influence this" and "I can't control this." Put your energy into the first column and practice letting the second go for now.

## Pressure from work or study
Plan your day with one to three priorities. Work in focused blocks (for example 25 minutes) followed by a short rest. Protect time to eat, sleep and relax.

## When to seek more support
If stress feels constant or is affecting your health, work or relationships, consider talking to a doctor, counsellor or other qualified professional.

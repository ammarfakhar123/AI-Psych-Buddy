# Coping With Anxious Moments

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## What anxious moments can feel like
Racing thoughts, a tight chest, restlessness or constant "what if" thinking are common when we feel under pressure. These feelings are uncomfortable but usually pass.

## Ideas to try in the moment
- **Name it.** Saying "I'm feeling anxious right now" can create a little distance from the feeling.
- **Ground yourself.** Try the 5-4-3-2-1 exercise to bring attention to the present.
- **Slow your breathing.** Breathe in for 4 and out for 6 a few times.
- **Write down the worry.** Then ask, "What is a realistic outcome? What is one thing I can do about it?"
- **Move.** A short walk or stretching can release some nervous energy.

## Worry time
Set aside 10 minutes each day as "worry time." When worries appear at other times, note them down and postpone them until then.

## Seeking support
If anxious feelings are frequent, intense or get in the way of daily life, a qualified professional can offer proper guidance and support.

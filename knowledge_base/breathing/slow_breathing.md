# Slow Breathing Exercises

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## Why slow breathing
Many people find that slowing and lengthening their breathing helps them feel calmer and more settled. Results vary from person to person, and it is fine to stop if it feels uncomfortable.

## Box breathing (4-4-4-4)
Breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds. Hold gently for 4 seconds. Breathe out slowly for 4 seconds. Pause for 4 seconds. Repeat for a few minutes.

## 4-6 breathing
Breathe in for 4 seconds and breathe out for 6 seconds. A longer out-breath is a gentle way to slow your pace. Try it for one to three minutes.

## Belly breathing
Place one hand on your belly. Breathe in slowly so your belly rises, then breathe out and feel it fall. Keep your shoulders relaxed.

## Gentle reminders
Breathe at a comfortable pace and never force or strain. If you feel dizzy, return to normal breathing. If you have a breathing-related health condition, check with your doctor before trying new breathing techniques.

# Sleep Hygiene Basics

*Educational wellness resource - not medical advice or treatment.*

## What sleep hygiene means
Sleep hygiene is a set of everyday habits that may support more restful sleep. Needs differ between people.

## Habits many people find helpful
- **Keep a regular schedule.** Go to bed and wake up around the same time each day.
- **Wind down.** Spend 30-60 minutes before bed doing something calm: reading, gentle stretching or soft music.
- **Limit screens at night.** Try to put phones away before bed, or dim the brightness.
- **Watch caffeine and heavy meals.** Avoid caffeine late in the day and large meals right before bed.
- **Make the bedroom comfortable.** Cool, dark and quiet works well for many people.
- **Get daylight and movement.** Morning light and daytime activity may help your body clock.

## If you can't fall asleep
Rather than lying awake frustrated, get up, do something quiet in dim light, and return when you feel sleepy. Writing a "worry list" earlier in the evening can help get thoughts out of your head.

## When to talk to a professional
If sleep problems last for weeks or strongly affect your daytime life, speak to a doctor or qualified professional.
